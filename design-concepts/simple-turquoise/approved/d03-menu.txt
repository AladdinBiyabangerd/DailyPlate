Approved structure: compact seven-day selector, real dates, selected check and
separate Today marker; illustrated cards with slot, name, status, calculated
snapshot energy and exact-version time when known. Recipe opens details; Ate
opens the existing portion editor and never writes without preview/confirmation.
Consumed/skipped items do not offer another intake action. Partial items retain
remaining-serving safeguards in the existing editor.

Approved temporary adaptations: generic existing artwork labelled illustration
until real recipe photos exist; no made-up energy/time. Replacement and shopping
are explicitly unavailable in one information block, with no inert fake actions.
Empty accepted-plan state explains content availability, preserving diary use.
Existing local content/publication gates remain binding; no sample production plan.
Fixture values in tests are labelled TEST and never enabled in the live catalog.

Native adaptations: existing navigation remains (D06 separately open); existing
Menu shell heading may remain above weekly content. Existing profile/plan controls
remain below the menu so versioned generation/retry/acceptance is preserved.
At standard text the compact selector scrolls horizontally to preserve Android
48dp targets on a 360dp display. All seven dates are reachable. At large text or
long RU labels, selector uses wrapping full-date day buttons. At RU 150% each
date uses full width to avoid breaking long weekday names inside a word;
card image sits above the text and actions stack to keep 48dp targets and labels.
Cards for all 3/4/5 configured slots remain scrollable. Week navigation only lists
persisted accepted plans; no invented week is generated by selecting a date.
Menu loader/errors, missing profile, no plan and no meals for selected date stay
explicit. The HTML proposal is a design reference, not runtime evidence.
